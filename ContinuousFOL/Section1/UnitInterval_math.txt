**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 5, Definition 2.4 (paraphrase)**

Truth values live in $[0,1]$: negation is interpreted as $x\mapsto 1-x$ and, in continuous logic, the extra connective $\tfrac12$ as $x\mapsto x/2$. Both maps send the unit interval into itself.

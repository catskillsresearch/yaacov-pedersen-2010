**Definition** (`CLPropForm.eval`). $v(P_i)=v_0(i)$, $v(\varphi\dot-\psi)=v(\varphi)\dot-v(\psi)$, $v(\neg\varphi)=1-v(\varphi)$ and $v(\tfrac12\varphi)=v(\varphi)/2$.

**Lemma** (`eval_mem_Icc`). If $v_0$ takes values in $[0,1]$, so does $v$.

*Proof.* Induction on the formula. Variables take the values of $v_0$, which lie in $[0,1]$. If $x,y\in[0,1]$, then $\max(x-y,0)\in[0,1]$ and $1-x\in[0,1]$. The case new to continuous logic is $\tfrac12\alpha$, and $x/2\in[0,\tfrac12]\subseteq[0,1]$ for $x\in[0,1]$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 5, Definition 2.4 (paraphrase)**

Truth assignments for continuous logic are computed exactly as for Łukasiewicz logic, with the additional clause that $\tfrac12\varphi$ receives half the value of $\varphi$.

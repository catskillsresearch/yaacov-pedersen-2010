**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 4, Definition 2.2 (paraphrase)**

A map $v_0$ from the propositional symbols to $[0,1]$ extends uniquely to all formulae: the value of $\varphi\dot-\psi$ is the truncated difference of the values, and the value of $\neg\varphi$ is one minus the value of $\varphi$. This extension is the truth assignment determined by $v_0$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 4, Definition 2.1 (paraphrase)**

Fix a set of distinct propositional symbols $P_i$, indexed by $i\in I$. A Łukasiewicz propositional logic is the set of formulae freely generated from these symbols by one binary connective $\dot-$ and one unary connective $\neg$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Remark 3.4 (paraphrase)**

Starting from $1$ and closing under $\neg$, $\dot-$ and $\tfrac12$ produces formulae whose values do not depend on the assignment; these values are exactly the dyadic rationals in $[0,1]$, and the paper identifies such formulae with their values.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 5, Axioms (A5)–(A6) (paraphrase)**

Continuous logic adds two schemata about halving to (A1)–(A4): (A5) $\tfrac12\varphi$ is at most $\varphi\dot-\tfrac12\varphi$, and (A6) $\varphi\dot-\tfrac12\varphi$ is at most $\tfrac12\varphi$. Together they force $\tfrac12\varphi$ to be exactly half of $\varphi$ under the intended semantics.

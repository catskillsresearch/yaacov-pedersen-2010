**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Notation 3.3 (paraphrase)**

There is no constant symbol for falsity; instead $1$ abbreviates $\neg(\varphi\dot-\varphi)$ for an arbitrary formula $\varphi$, which every assignment evaluates to $1$. Then $0$ abbreviates $\neg1$, and $2^{-n}$ is $1$ halved $n$ times.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Remark 3.4 (paraphrase)**

Whatever the truth assignment, the values taken by the expressions in $\mathcal D$ form exactly the set $\mathbb D$ of dyadic numbers $k/2^n$ with $k\le 2^n$. The paper therefore does not distinguish between such expressions and the dyadic numbers they denote.

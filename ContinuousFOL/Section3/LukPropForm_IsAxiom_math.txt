**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 5, Axioms (A1)–(A4) (paraphrase)**

Four schemata axiomatize Łukasiewicz logic. Reading $\psi\dot-\varphi$ as "$\psi$ is at most $\varphi$": (A1) $\varphi\dot-\psi$ is at most $\varphi$; (A2) $(\chi\dot-\varphi)\dot-(\chi\dot-\psi)$ is at most $\psi\dot-\varphi$; (A3) $\varphi\dot-(\varphi\dot-\psi)$ is at most $\psi\dot-(\psi\dot-\varphi)$ (both express the minimum); (A4) $\varphi\dot-\psi$ is at most $\neg\psi\dot-\neg\varphi$ (contraposition).

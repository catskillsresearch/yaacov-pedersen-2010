**Theorem** (`cl_approx_strong_completeness`). For any set $\Sigma$ and formula $\varphi$ of continuous propositional logic, $\Sigma\models\varphi$ iff $\Sigma\vdash\varphi\dot-2^{-n}$ for every $n\in\mathbb N$.

*Proof.* This is `entails_iff_forall_provable_tsub_pow2Neg`: soundness and a limit argument in one direction, and compactness plus finite strong completeness in the other.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 7, Fact 4.4 (paraphrase)**

In continuous propositional logic, $\Sigma\models\varphi$ holds exactly when, for every $n$, $\Sigma$ proves that $\varphi$ is at most $2^{-n}$, i.e. proves $\varphi\dot-2^{-n}$.

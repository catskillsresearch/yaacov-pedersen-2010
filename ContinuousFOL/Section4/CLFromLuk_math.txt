**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 7, Fact 4.5 (paraphrase)**

For finite premise sets continuous propositional logic is strongly complete: if finitely many premises semantically force $\varphi$, then $\varphi$ is derivable from them.

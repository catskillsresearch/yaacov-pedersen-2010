**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 16, Theorem 8.1 (paraphrase)**

The same weak deduction theorem holds in continuous propositional logic: a hypothesis can be discharged at the cost of subtracting it finitely many times from the conclusion.

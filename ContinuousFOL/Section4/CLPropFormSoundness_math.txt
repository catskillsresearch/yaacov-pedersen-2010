**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Axioms (A5)–(A6) (paraphrase)**

Continuous propositional logic is sound for its truth assignments: provability from $\Sigma$ implies truth in all models of $\Sigma$, for arbitrary (also infinite) $\Sigma$.

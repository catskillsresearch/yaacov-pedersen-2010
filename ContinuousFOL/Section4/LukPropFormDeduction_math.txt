**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 16, Theorem 8.1 (paraphrase)**

The paper's deduction theorem (stated for continuous first-order logic, with the propositional case implicit in §4) says: adding a hypothesis $\psi$ lets us prove $\varphi$ exactly when, without it, we can prove $\varphi$ minus some finite number of copies of $\psi$. Its proof rests on a Łukasiewicz tautology that lets two such iterated subtractions be combined, followed by induction on the derivation.

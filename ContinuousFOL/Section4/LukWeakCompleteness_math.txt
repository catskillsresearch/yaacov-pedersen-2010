**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Fact 4.1 (paraphrase)**

A Łukasiewicz formula is valid (value $0$ under every assignment) if and only if it is provable from no premises; the paper cites Chang and Rose–Rosser.

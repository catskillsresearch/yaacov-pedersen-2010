**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Fact 4.1 (paraphrase)**

Weak completeness of Łukasiewicz logic is attributed to Chang and to Rose–Rosser. Chang's proof is algebraic: the logic is modelled by MV-algebras, and every MV-algebra that satisfies an equation also has it satisfied in the real interval $[0,1]$.

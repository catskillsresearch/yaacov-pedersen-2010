**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 4, Definition 2.2 (paraphrase)**

The intended semantics evaluates formulae in $[0,1]$ with $\dot-$ as truncated subtraction and $\neg$ as $1-x$. In algebraic terms this is the standard MV-algebra on the unit interval, with $x\oplus y=\min(x+y,1)$.

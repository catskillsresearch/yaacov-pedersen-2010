**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 13, Definition 6.10 (paraphrase)**

For a signature with a metric, $\Gamma$ is completely satisfiable if it has a model that is a genuine continuous structure (complete metric), and $\Gamma\models_{QC}\varphi$ if every such model of $\Gamma$ makes $\varphi$ zero.

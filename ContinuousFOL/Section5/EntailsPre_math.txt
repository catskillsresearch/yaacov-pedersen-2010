**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 11, Definition 5.13 (paraphrase)**

$\Gamma\models_Q\varphi$ means that $\varphi$ is $0$ in every model of $\Gamma$; a formula entailed by the empty set is valid.

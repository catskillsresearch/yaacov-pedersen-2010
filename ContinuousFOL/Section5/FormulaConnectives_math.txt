**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 8, §5 abbreviations (paraphrase)**

Abbreviations: $\inf_x\varphi:=\neg\sup_x\neg\varphi$, $\varphi\wedge\psi:=\varphi\dot-(\varphi\dot-\psi)$, $\varphi\vee\psi:=\neg(\neg\varphi\wedge\neg\psi)$ and $|\varphi-\psi|:=(\varphi\dot-\psi)\vee(\psi\dot-\varphi)$. They are interpreted by infimum, minimum, maximum and absolute difference.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Notation 3.3 (paraphrase)**

The constant $1$ is written as $\neg(\varphi\dot-\varphi)$, which requires some formula $\varphi$ to exist; in first-order logic an atomic formula is always available because there is at least one relation symbol.

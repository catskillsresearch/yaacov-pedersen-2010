**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 6, Remark 3.4 (paraphrase)**

Starting from $1$ and closing under the connectives one obtains formulae whose values are exactly the dyadic rationals in $[0,1]$; in particular $2^{-n}$ is $\tfrac12$ applied $n$ times to $1$.

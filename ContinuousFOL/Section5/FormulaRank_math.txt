**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 21, Definition 9.3 (paraphrase)**

The rank counts connectives and quantifiers: atomic formulae have rank $0$, each unary connective or quantifier adds one, and $\varphi\dot-\psi$ has rank $\mathrm{rank}\,\varphi+\mathrm{rank}\,\psi+1$.

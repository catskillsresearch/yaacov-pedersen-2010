**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 8, Definition 5.4 (paraphrase)**

Formulae are built from atomic formulae $Pt_0\cdots t_{n-1}$ with the connectives $\dot-$, $\neg$, $\tfrac12$ and the quantifier $\sup_x$, as in classical first-order logic.

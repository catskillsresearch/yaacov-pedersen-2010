**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 9, Definition 5.6 (paraphrase)**

For a signature with a metric, $d$ must be interpreted as a pseudo-metric with values in $[0,1]$, and every symbol must respect its moduli: if $c$ and $e$ are closer than $\delta_{s,i}(\epsilon)$ then replacing $c$ by $e$ in argument $i$ moves a function value by at most $\epsilon$ in $d$, and increases a relation value by at most $\epsilon$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 9, Remark 5.8 (paraphrase)**

These continuity requirements replace the congruence axioms for equality in classical logic; consequently every symbol is interpreted by a uniformly continuous map.

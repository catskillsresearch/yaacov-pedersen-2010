**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 8, Definition 5.4 (paraphrase)**

A signature with a metric singles out one binary relation symbol $d$. Unlike equality in classical logic, $d$ is a relation symbol, so $d\,t_0t_1$ is a formula with a truth value, not a term.

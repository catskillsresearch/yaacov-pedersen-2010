**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 11, Definition 5.12 (paraphrase)**

A pair of a pre-structure and an assignment is a model of $\Gamma$ when every formula of $\Gamma$ takes the value $0$ (which plays the role of "true"); $\Gamma$ is satisfiable when it has a model.

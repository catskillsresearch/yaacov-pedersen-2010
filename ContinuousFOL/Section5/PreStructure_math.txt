**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 9, Definition 5.6 (paraphrase)**

A continuous $\mathcal L$-pre-structure is a non-empty set $M$ in which every $n$-ary function symbol is interpreted by a map $M^n\to M$ and every $n$-ary relation symbol by a map $M^n\to[0,1]$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 10, Definition 5.10 (paraphrase)**

An assignment maps variables into the structure; $\sigma^a_x$ is the assignment that sends $x$ to $a$ and agrees with $\sigma$ elsewhere.

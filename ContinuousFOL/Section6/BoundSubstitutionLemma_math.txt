**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 12, Lemma 6.4 (paraphrase)**

Given finitely many variables, a formula can be transformed by bound renamings into an equivalent formula in which none of them is bound. The paper calls this immediate and refers to classical textbooks.

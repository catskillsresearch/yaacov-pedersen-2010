**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 13, Corollary 6.11 (paraphrase)**

For signatures with a metric, nothing changes if one restricts to complete structures: entailment over pre-structures equals entailment over complete structures, and satisfiability equals complete satisfiability.

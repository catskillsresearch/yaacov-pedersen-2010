**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 11, Definition 6.3 (paraphrase)**

$\varphi\{y/x\}$ renames the bound variable $x$ to $y$: each subformula $\sup_x\alpha$ becomes $\sup_y\alpha[y/x]$. The renaming is correct when $y$ is not free in $\alpha$ and the inner substitution is correct.

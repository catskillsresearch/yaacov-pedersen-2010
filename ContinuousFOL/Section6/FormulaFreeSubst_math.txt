**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 11, Definition 6.1 (paraphrase)**

$\varphi[t/x]$ replaces the free occurrences of $x$ in $\varphi$ by $t$; it is correct when no variable of $t$ becomes bound by a quantifier in the result.

**Definitions.** $\mathrm{FV}(P\bar t)=\bigcup\mathrm{vars}(t_i)$, connectives take unions, and $\mathrm{FV}(\sup_x\alpha)=\mathrm{FV}(\alpha)\setminus\{x\}$. $\mathrm{BV}$ collects the variables bound by some $\sup$, and $\mathrm{AV}$ all variables occurring at all.

**Definition** (`supClosure`). List the free variables of $\varphi$ in increasing order $x_1<\dots<x_k$ and put $\overline\varphi:=\sup_{x_1}\cdots\sup_{x_k}\varphi$.

**Lemma.** $\overline\varphi$ has no free variables, and $\overline\varphi=\varphi$ when $\varphi$ is already a sentence.

*Proof.* Since $\mathrm{FV}(\sup_x\alpha)=\mathrm{FV}(\alpha)\setminus\{x\}$, induction on a list $x_1,\dots,x_k$ shows $\mathrm{FV}(\sup_{x_1}\cdots\sup_{x_k}\varphi)=\mathrm{FV}(\varphi)\setminus\{x_1,\dots,x_k\}$. For the list of all free variables of $\varphi$ this set is empty. If $\varphi$ is a sentence the list is empty, so nothing is prefixed and $\overline\varphi=\varphi$.

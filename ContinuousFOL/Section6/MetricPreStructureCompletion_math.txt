**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 13, Theorem 6.9 (paraphrase)**

Every pre-structure for a metric signature maps elementarily into a complete continuous structure for the same signature. The key fact is that a map with a modulus of uniform continuity, defined on a subset of a metric space and valued in a complete space, extends uniquely to the closure with the same modulus; so the moduli of the signature survive the completion.

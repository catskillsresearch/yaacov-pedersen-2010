**Lemma.** If $x\ne y$ then $(\sigma^a_y)^v_x=(\sigma^v_x)^a_y$.

*Proof.* Compare the two assignments at a variable $z$. At $z=x$ both give $v$, and at $z=y$ both give $a$; this uses $x\ne y$, so that neither update overwrites the other. At any other $z$ both give $\sigma(z)$.

**Lemma** (term substitution). $(s[t/x])^{\mathfrak M,\sigma}=s^{\mathfrak M,\sigma^a_x}$ with $a=t^{\mathfrak M,\sigma}$.

*Proof.* Induction on $s$. If $s$ is the variable $x$, the left side is the value $a$ of $t$, and the right side is $\sigma^a_x(x)=a$. If $s$ is another variable $y$, substitution leaves it unchanged and both sides are $\sigma(y)$. If $s=f(s_1,\dots,s_n)$, substitution acts on each argument, so both sides apply $f^{\mathfrak M}$ to equal arguments by the induction hypothesis.

**Lemma** (coincidence). If $y\notin\mathrm{vars}(t)$ then $t^{\mathfrak M,\sigma^a_y}=t^{\mathfrak M,\sigma}$; if $x\notin\mathrm{FV}(\varphi)$ then $\mathfrak M(\varphi,\sigma^a_x)=\mathfrak M(\varphi,\sigma)$.

*Proof.* Induction on $t$, resp. $\varphi$; in the case $\sup_z\alpha$ with $z\ne x$, swap the updates at $x$ and $z$ and use the induction hypothesis pointwise inside the supremum, while for $z=x$ the outer update is overwritten.

**Theorem** (`substitution_lemma`, Lemma 6.2). If $\varphi[t/x]$ is correct, then $\mathfrak M(\varphi[t/x],\sigma)=\mathfrak M(\varphi,\sigma^a_x)$ with $a=t^{\mathfrak M,\sigma}$.

*Proof.* Induction on $\varphi$, for all $\sigma$. Atomic formulae: the term lemma. Connectives: immediate. For $\sup_x\alpha$ the substitution does nothing, and $x$ is not free, so the coincidence lemma applies. For $\sup_y\alpha$ with $y\ne x$: if $x\notin\mathrm{FV}(\alpha)$ the substitution is invisible semantically and both sides equal $\mathfrak M(\sup_y\alpha,\sigma)$ by coincidence. Otherwise correctness gives $y\notin\mathrm{vars}(t)$, so $t$ has the same value $a$ under every $\sigma^b_y$; applying the induction hypothesis at $\sigma^b_y$ and commuting the updates shows that the families whose suprema are taken coincide.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 11, Lemma 6.2 (paraphrase)**

If $\varphi[t/x]$ is a correct substitution, then evaluating it under $\sigma$ is the same as evaluating $\varphi$ under the assignment that sends $x$ to the value of $t$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 14, Axioms (A7)–(A9) (paraphrase)**

The quantifier schemata imitate classical ones: (A7) $\sup$ distributes over $\dot-$ in the sense that $(\sup_x\psi\dot-\sup_x\varphi)\dot-\sup_x(\psi\dot-\varphi)$ is an axiom; (A8) a correct instance $\varphi[t/x]$ is below $\sup_x\varphi$; and (A9) $\sup_x\varphi$ is below $\varphi$ when $x$ is not free in $\varphi$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 14, §7 generalizations (paraphrase)**

A generalization of $\psi$ is any formula $\sup_{x_1}\cdots\sup_{x_n}\psi$ with $n\ge0$; in particular $\psi$ is a generalization of itself. Every generalization of an axiom instance is itself an axiom.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 14, §7 provability (paraphrase)**

$\Gamma\vdash_Q\varphi$ means $\varphi$ has a deduction from $\Gamma$ whose axioms are generalizations of instances of the schemata, with modus ponens as the only rule.

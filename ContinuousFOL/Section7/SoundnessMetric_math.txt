**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 15, Theorem 7.2 (paraphrase)**

For a signature with a metric, soundness holds with respect to pre-structures and to complete structures; the validity of (A10)–(A14) is exactly the discussion following (A14): the pseudo-metric axioms hold because $d$ is a pseudo-metric, and (A13)–(A14) hold because the symbols obey their moduli.

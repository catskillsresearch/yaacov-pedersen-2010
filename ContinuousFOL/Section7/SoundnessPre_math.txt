**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 15, Theorem 7.2 (paraphrase)**

Soundness: provable consequences are semantic consequences, and a satisfiable set is consistent. It suffices that every axiom is valid: (A1)–(A6) by the propositional facts, (A7)–(A9) by the substitution results of §6.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 18, Lemma 8.5 (paraphrase)**

Two ways to extend a consistent set: (i) a formula that the set proves to be below every $2^{-n}$ can be added consistently; (ii) for any two formulae, one of the two comparisons $\varphi\dot-\psi$, $\psi\dot-\varphi$ can be added consistently.

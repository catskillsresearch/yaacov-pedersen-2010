**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 16, Theorem 8.1 (paraphrase)**

Adding $\psi$ as a premise proves $\varphi$ exactly when, without $\psi$, one can prove $\varphi\dot-n\psi$ for some $n$. The proof rests on a Łukasiewicz tautology that lets the number of subtractions of $\psi$ add up along modus ponens.

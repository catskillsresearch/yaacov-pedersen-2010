**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 16, Lemma 8.2 (paraphrase)**

If $\Gamma$ proves $\varphi$ and $x$ is not free in any premise, then $\Gamma$ proves $\sup_x\varphi$; the argument is the classical one.

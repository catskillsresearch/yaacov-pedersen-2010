**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 16, Lemma 8.3 (paraphrase)**

If $y$ does not occur in $\varphi$, then $\varphi\dot-\varphi\{y/x\}$ and $\varphi\{y/x\}\dot-\varphi$ are both provable. The paper proves this by induction on $\varphi$, using propositional tautologies from §4 for the connectives and (A7)–(A9) with generalization for the quantifier step.

**Theorem** (`approximated_strong_completeness_pre`). $\Gamma\models_Q\varphi$ iff $\Gamma\vdash_Q\varphi\dot-2^{-n}$ for all $n$.

*Proof.* ($\Leftarrow$) By soundness every model gives $\varphi$ a value at most $2^{-n}$ for all $n$, hence $0$. ($\Rightarrow$) Fix $n$. Every model of $\Gamma$ gives $\varphi$ the value $0$, so no model makes $2^{-n}\dot-\varphi$ zero, i.e. $\Gamma\cup\{2^{-n}\dot-\varphi\}$ is unsatisfiable. By completeness it is inconsistent, and the refutation lemma gives $\Gamma\vdash\varphi\dot-2^{-n}$.

**Theorems** (`..._metric_pre`, `..._metric_complete`, `approximated_strong_completeness_metric`). The same equivalence for metric pre-structures and for complete structures (using complete satisfiability in the completeness step).

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 24, Corollary 9.6 (paraphrase)**

$\Gamma\models\varphi$ iff $\Gamma\vdash\varphi\dot-2^{-n}$ for every $n$, both over pre-structures and, with a metric, over complete structures. Exact strong completeness fails, so this is the best one can hope for.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 25, Corollary 9.12 (paraphrase)**

A set of formulae all of whose finite subsets are (completely) satisfiable is itself (completely) satisfiable; the paper notes that ultraproducts would also give this.

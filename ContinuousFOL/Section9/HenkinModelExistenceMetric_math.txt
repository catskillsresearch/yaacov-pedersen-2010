**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 21, Theorem 9.4 (paraphrase)**

The Henkin model theorem holds also for signatures with a metric; then the model is a pre-structure for the metric signature, i.e. $d$ is a pseudo-metric and the moduli are respected.

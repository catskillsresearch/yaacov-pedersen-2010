**Theorem** (`henkin_model_existence`). If $\Gamma$ is consistent there are a signature extension $\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal L^c$, a maximal consistent Henkin complete $\Delta\subseteq\mathrm{For}(\mathcal L^c)$ containing the image of $\Gamma$, and a pre-structure $\mathfrak M$ (in universe $0$) with an assignment $\sigma$ such that $(\mathfrak M,\sigma)\models_Q\Delta$.

*Proof.* Proposition 9.2 gives a consistent Henkin complete $e[\Gamma]\cup\Gamma^c$; Theorem 8.10 extends it to a maximal consistent $\Delta$, which is still Henkin complete since it contains the Henkin axioms. Take the term pre-structure and the identity assignment. If $\varphi\in\Delta$ then $\varphi\dot-0\in\Delta$ by (A1), so $0\in B(\varphi)$ and, by the truth lemma and Lemma 8.12, $\mathfrak M(\varphi,\sigma)=\sup A(\varphi)=\inf B(\varphi)=0$.

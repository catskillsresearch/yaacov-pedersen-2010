**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 21, Theorem 9.4 (paraphrase)**

A consistent set of formulae extends, in a larger signature, to a maximal consistent Henkin complete set that is satisfied by a pre-structure and an assignment.

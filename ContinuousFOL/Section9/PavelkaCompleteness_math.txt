**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 24, Corollary 9.8 (paraphrase)**

Degree of truth equals degree of provability. One inequality is soundness; for the other, if $p$ is below the degree of provability, $\Gamma$ does not prove $\varphi\dot-p$, so $\Gamma\cup\{p\dot-\varphi\}$ is consistent and has a model in which $\varphi\ge p$.

**Definition** (`SignatureExtension`). A bijection of relation symbols and an injection of function symbols from $\mathcal L$ to $\mathcal L'$ preserving arities and moduli.

**Definition.** $e(t)$ and $e(\varphi)$ rename the symbols of a term or formula along $e$. For an $\mathcal L'$-pre-structure $\mathfrak N$, the reduct $\mathfrak N|_{\mathcal L}$ has the same carrier and interprets each $\mathcal L$-symbol as its image does in $\mathfrak N$.

**Lemma** (`eval_reduct`). $t^{\mathfrak N|_{\mathcal L},\sigma}=e(t)^{\mathfrak N,\sigma}$ and $\mathfrak N|_{\mathcal L}(\varphi,\sigma)=\mathfrak N(e(\varphi),\sigma)$.

*Proof.* Terms by induction: variables have the same value on both sides. For $f(t_1,\dots,t_n)$ the reduct interprets $f$ as $\mathfrak N$ interprets $e(f)$, and the arguments agree by the induction hypothesis. Formulae by induction: atomic formulae use the term case and the same convention for relation symbols, and the connectives act on equal values. For $\sup_x\alpha$ both sides are suprema over the same carrier of the values at $\sigma^a_x$, which agree pointwise by the induction hypothesis.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 21, Theorem 9.4 (paraphrase)**

The heart of the proof: by induction on rank, the value of every formula in the term model equals $\sup\{p\in\mathbb D:\ p\dot-\varphi\in\Delta\}$. The paper writes out the cases $\tfrac12\psi$ and $\sup_x\psi$. For the quantifier, a gap below the cut is closed by a Henkin witness, and a gap above it by Lemma 8.11 and the Substitution Lemma.

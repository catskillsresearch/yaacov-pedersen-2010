**Definition** (`termPreStructure`). For a set of formulae $\Delta$: the carrier is the set of terms (nonempty, it contains $v_0$); $f^{\mathfrak M}(\bar t)=f\bar t$; and $P^{\mathfrak M}(\bar t)=\sup\{p\in\mathbb D:\ p\dot-P\bar t\in\Delta\}$, which lies in $[0,1]$ (the supremum of a nonempty set of dyadics in $[0,1]$, or $0$ for the empty set).

**Lemma.** With $\sigma(x)=x$, $t^{\mathfrak M,\sigma}=t$ for every term $t$, by induction on $t$.

**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 21, Theorem 9.4 (paraphrase)**

The Henkin model has the terms as elements; function symbols build terms, and a relation $P$ at terms $\bar t$ gets the value $\sup\{p\in\mathbb D:\ p\dot-P\bar t\in\Delta\}$. The assignment sends each variable to itself, so every term denotes itself.

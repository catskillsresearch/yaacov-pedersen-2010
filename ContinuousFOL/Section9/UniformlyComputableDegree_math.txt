**Ben Yaacov–Pedersen (2010), arXiv p. 25, Corollary 9.11 (paraphrase)**

A complete theory with a recursive or r.e. axiomatization is decidable: the degree of truth of a sentence can be approximated to any precision by searching for proofs of matching upper and lower dyadic bounds, which exist by approximated strong completeness.
